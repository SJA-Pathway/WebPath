# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SJA Pathway is committed to providing a respectful and inclusive environment for all contributors.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Be welcoming and helpful to new users
- Support a positive learning environment
- Respect others opinions and skill levels
- Communicate clearly and constructively

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Toxic or disrespectful attacks
- Disruptive behavior in community discussions or pull requests

---

## Enforcement

SJA Pathway may:

- Issue warnings
- Temporarily restrict access
- Remove contributors if necessary

---

## Reporting Issues

Report issues to SJA privately.

---

We aim to maintain a safe and collaborative environment for everyone.
